Frequently asked questions

Can I stop a lock early?
Generally, no. A lock can't be removed until it ends. Only when you really need to, you can pay to unlock from Unlock in the app. Your first unlock after installing is free.

Why can't I delete apps while locked?
This prevents getting around the lock. iOS doesn't allow limiting this to Kakugo Lock alone, so no apps on the device can be deleted while locked. This returns to normal when the lock ends.

I loosened a setting, but it hasn't taken effect.
Changes that loosen your settings, such as removing blocked items or turning off a schedule, take effect from midnight the next day.

The Unlock button doesn't appear on the lock screen.
It only appears when notifications are allowed. Allow notifications for Kakugo Lock in the Settings app, or open Kakugo Lock directly to unlock.
