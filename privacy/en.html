Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026
Operator: NyProject
Contact: nyproject0123@gmail.com

1. Overview

Kakugo Lock ("the app") works without an account. Your lock settings are stored only on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Which apps, categories, or websites you chose to block (iOS does not let the app read them)
  • How long you use each app, or your browsing history
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA). The app shows no ads

3. Information we collect (only if you choose to share)

Only if you choose "Share" during setup, or turn sharing on in Settings, we collect the following to improve the app. If you choose "Don't Share", none of it is collected.

  • Usage data: starting and extending locks (type and length), changes to schedules and the daily limit, use of unlocks (type, free or paid, price tier), and completing setup
  • Crash reports: what happened when the app quit unexpectedly
  • Device information: general information such as model, OS version, language, and country or region
  • An identifier assigned to each installation of the app (it does not identify you personally)

4. How we use it

We use this information only to fix problems and improve features. We do not use it for advertising or sell it to third parties.

5. Third-party services

We use Firebase by Google LLC (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics) to collect and store this information. It is processed on Google's servers, which may be outside your country.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Purchases

Paid unlocks are processed through Apple's in-app purchase system. The app does not collect payment details such as credit card numbers.

7. Changing your choice

You can turn sharing on or off at any time in the app's Settings. Once off, nothing further is collected.

8. Information stored on your device

Lock settings, schedules, and the daily limit are stored on your device. Whether you have used your free unlock and how many times you unlocked today are stored in an area that remains after the app is deleted (the keychain). None of this is sent anywhere.

9. Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
